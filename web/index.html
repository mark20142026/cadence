Level

Space, Up, W or click to jump. R restarts.

Your game files stay on this device: the page reads only the sprite sheets, icons and levels it needs, and never uploads them. Online levels, songs and (if you log in) your account requests go to the game's servers through this site's relay, which passes them on without storing or logging anything.
